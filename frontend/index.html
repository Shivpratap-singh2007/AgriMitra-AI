THE PROGRAM

Your campus. Your voice. Your opportunity.

CampusConnect Ambassadors are passionate students who bring meaningful opportunities, events, and ideas to their college communities.

As an ambassador, you will represent our organisation, build an active student network, and gain hands-on experience in communication, leadership, marketing, and community building.

QUESTIONS?

We've got answers.

Who can apply?

Any enthusiastic college or university student can apply. You do not need prior ambassador experience.

How long is the program?

The program runs for six months, with flexible activities designed around your academic schedule.

Is there any application fee?

No. Applying to and participating in the program is completely free.

How are ambassadors selected?

We review your application, motivation, and ideas for creating impact on your campus.

How much time does it require?

Most ambassadors spend two to four hours per week, depending on their campus goals and activities.
